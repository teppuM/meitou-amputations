[b]Requires MeitouClient 0.22.1 or newer ([url=https://meitou.kenshi.wiki]meitou.kenshi.wiki[/url]). The original Kenshi ignores this mod.[/b]

Adds surgery: two operating tables where your surgeon removes a squad member's limb on purpose, for instance to make room for a better prosthetic.

[list]
[*][b]Operating Table[/b], for people. Unlocked by the research [b]Surgery[/b] (level 4, after Medicine, costs an Ancient Science Book). Heals like a bed.
[*][b]Skeleton Operating Bed[/b], for skeletons. Unlocked by the research [b]Skeleton Operating Beds[/b] (level 4, after Skeleton Repair Beds, costs Engineering Research). Repairs faster than a Skeleton Repair Bed and, like it, needs power.
[*]Both are indoor furniture, built inside a building you own like any bed.
[/list]

[b]How to operate[/b]
[list]
[*]Put the patient on the table (right-click the table and use it, as with a bed).
[*]Select your surgeon, right-click the patient and choose [b]Amputate...[/b], then pick Left Arm, Right Arm, Left Leg or Right Leg. A limb that is already gone is greyed out.
[*]The surgeon walks over and works at the table for a few seconds under an "Amputating" progress bar. Then the limb comes off and drops to the ground, and the stump is left already tended.
[/list]

[b]Skill matters[/b]
[list]
[*]The surgeon's First Aid (Robotics on a skeleton) decides how much blood (or oil) the patient loses: about a fifth of it for a master surgeon, about three fifths for a beginner.
[*]A beginner usually botches the job and says so. A botched operation costs two and a half times as much, which can knock the patient out, and a second botched operation can kill.
[*]The surgeon gains skill experience from each operation, and the patient toughness, as from any lost limb.
[*]A skeleton has to go on the Skeleton Operating Bed and a person on the Operating Table; the surgeon tells you if the patient is on the wrong one.
[/list]
